**Comments:** zero viewer comments on 70K views is far below the norm (a study of 16K Shorts found about 0.6 comments per 1,000 views). I checked Studio: comments are on and nothing is being blocked (only 1 spam comment held). Viewers simply are not asked something they can answer in one tap.

**Subscribers:** channels that grow subscribers fast run **series with a recurring cast or numbered seasons** (Earclacks, MoreBalls "Season 26, Race 18", MIKAN "Escape from Me 3"), not one-off videos.

**Policy (July 2025 and July 2026 updates):** content that "looks like it's made with a template" is not monetizable, and enforcement can look at a channel's last ~30 uploads. Varying the mechanic every upload, a visibly authored style and a one-line on-screen "why" after the answer protect us. Never use copyrighted songs or other creators' setups.

## 2. The concept portfolio (8 lanes, rotate them)

Each lane is a *structure*. Every video in a lane still needs a new mechanic (no copies).

| # | Lane | Example titles (ours, new) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Count until** | "How Many Hammer Hits Until the Wall Breaks?" / "How Many Coins Until the Giant Scale Tips?" | Domino Giant 53K; "until" titles 111K median; Bridge Load is next |
| 2 | **Time until / sync** | "When Do All 12 Pendulums Line Up Again?" / "How Long Until the Pressure Tank Bursts?" | BetOnTheColor 2.4–2.5M |
| 3 | **Destruction count** | "The Press Crushed One Cube… Into How Many Pieces?" / "One Shot Shatters the Glass Wall. How Many Shards?" | shatter/crush 579K median; OddlyCoded 2.9M |
| 4 | **Proliferation / survival** | "Every Ball That Escapes Becomes 2… How Many After 30 Seconds?" / "Can 1 Ball Survive 100 Spikes?" | MIKAN 28–84M hits; Satisfying Physics Simulation +3.9K subs in 30 days |
| 5 | **Predixel League (series)** | "Predixel League S1 E3: Ice Arena. Can Comet Keep First Place?" | series drive subscribers (Earclacks, MoreBalls) |
| 6 | **Merge to final form** (original objects: dust → rock → planet → star → black hole; tiles 2 → 2048) | "Will It Reach the Final Form? How Many Merges?" | merge 125K median, 2.4–5.9M hits |
| 7 | **Big roster** (50 US States, 100 countries) | "50 States Marble Race: Will Your State Survive Round 1?" | marble-flag 166K median; Riley Marbles 11.8M; drives "my state!" comments |
| 8 | **Music bounce** (public-domain tunes only) | "Every Bounce Plays a Note… Name the Song!" | note-per-bounce viral 38M; Bouncy Physics 645K |

**Predixel League (lane 5):**
- **Cast:** 6 named characters with fixed colors and symbols (for example Comet, Ember, Frost, Bolt, Moss, Nova).
- **Episodes:** each one is a different physics arena (ice, magnets, gravity flip, spinning drum…).
- **Season table:** points are shown in the end card.
- **Viewer input:** the most-liked comment picks the next arena.

This gives viewers a reason to subscribe ("who wins the season?") and to comment ("Go Ember!"), and every episode still has a new mechanic.

**Later capability bet:** realistic 3D destruction loops (Bounce Theory, Grid Loops) are the biggest category, with a 3.9M top 10%. They need free Blender rendering. Prototype one in December, after the 2D lanes are running.

## 3. Rules for the next 14 slots (one week)

- Mix:
  - **4** count-until or time-until
  - **2** destruction count
  - **2** proliferation or survival
  - **2** League episodes (start Season 1)
  - **2** merge to final form
  - **1** big roster (50 US States)
  - **1** music bounce (public domain)
- Never the same lane twice in a row.
- **Titles:** use "… until …" or "How many …" or a big number ("50 States", "100 Spikes"); name colors or characters where the video has them. Drop the plain "Which color wins?" wording (no lift).
- **Every Short:** event by 0.5 s, a running counter or score, answer in the last 2–3 s, one-line "why" after the answer, end card with "Got it right? +1 streak".
- **Comments:** first description line and pinned comment = one-tap answer ("Comment A, B, C or D 👇", "Comment your number 👇", "Comment your state 👇"). Reply to the first 10 comments on each Short.
- **Judge each lane after 3 Shorts:**
  - keep it if median stayed-to-watch ≥ 45% or views ≥ 3K
  - drop it below 30% / 1K
- **Plain color races** (Star Forge 20%, River Drift stalled at 361 views): stop.
