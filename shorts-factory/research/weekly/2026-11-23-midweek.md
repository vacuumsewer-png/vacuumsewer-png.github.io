**The pattern behind it (reuse the structure in new mechanics, never copy the video):**
1. **One question with a number for an answer** ("How many?"). Everyone can guess, nobody knows.
2. **Something happens on frame 0, then every half second**: each domino hit is a small event, and the counter ticks up. There is no setup stretch.
3. **Escalation you can see**: each step is bigger than the last, and the camera pulls back past a coin, a hand, a person and a house. Viewers stay to see how big it gets.
4. **A running counter on screen**: viewers compare it with their guess the whole time, which is the reason to keep watching.
5. **The answer comes in the last 2–3 s**, with a short end card.
6. **A title that is a clear question**: "Each Domino Is 1.5x Bigger… How Many to Topple a Tower? 🤔"
